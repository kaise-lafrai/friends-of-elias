Common questions

How do I add someone to my child's circle?

Open the child's page, tap the menu in the top corner, then Invite someone. Choose their role, then share the invite link or code with them. Each one works once and expires, so send it soon, and only to the person it's for.

I got a new phone. How do I get back in?

Sign in on the new phone with the same email. We'll send you a one-time code, and your circles will be there.

I logged something by mistake.

Tap the entry. For the first 15 minutes you can edit it or mark it as logged by mistake. After that, add a correction. The original stays visible so everyone can see what changed.

Why won't the app save my note?

To protect your family's privacy, the app blocks notes that look like phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, birth dates or ID numbers. Remove those details and try again.

How do I delete my account?

Email privacy@friendsofelias.com from the address on your account and we'll do it for you. See the privacy policy for what's removed and when.

Report a problem

Email support@friendsofelias.com with what you were doing, what you expected and what happened, plus your phone model. Screenshots help, but please cover up anything that identifies your child.
