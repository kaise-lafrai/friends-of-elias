Everyone who cares for a child, on the same page.

We're building a shared care log for families, nurses, teachers and everyone else in a child's day: what happened, what's due and what needs a follow-up, in one place.

Log it once

Meds, sleep, seizures, fluids and notes, logged in a couple of taps.

Hand off clearly

Whoever takes over sees what's due and what's still open.

Private by design

No ads, no tracking. The family decides who's in the circle and what they see.
