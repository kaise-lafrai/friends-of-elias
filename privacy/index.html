Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Friends of Elias, LLC ("we", "us") makes Everyhand, a care log app that helps a child's family and caregivers share what's happening in the child's day. Everyhand is in private beta on the web, with iPhone and Android apps on the way. This policy explains what information the app and our websites collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect as little as we can. You don't give us your name, and the app asks for a nickname for the child, not their real name. We don't sell your information, show ads or use tracking or advertising tools. Only the people a family invites into a child's circle can see that child's records.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address. You need an email to create an account. You sign in with it and a one-time code, so there's no password. People in your circles never see your email; they see only what the circle calls you. We don't ask for your name, phone number or address.
  • What you agreed to. The date you accepted our terms and this policy, whether you asked for news about new features and plans, and the wording you saw when you made that choice.

What your circle records

  • A nickname for the child (for example, "Bean") and display settings such as time zone.
  • Circle members' role labels, such as "Mom" or "Night nurse", along with each person's role and what they're allowed to see.
  • Care entries and plans that members add: medications and doses, sleep, seizures, feeding and fluids, toileting, moods, handoffs, notes, follow-ups and corrections, with the time, the setting and which member logged them.
  • Invites. We store only a scrambled (hashed) form of each invite code, never the code itself, along with the role it's for and when it expires. You share the code or invite link yourself, using your own messaging apps, so we never see who you send it to. Anyone who has an unused invite can use it, so share it only with the person it's for.
  • An activity record of who added, changed or opened records in a circle. We use it to investigate misuse and security problems. It isn't shown in the app. It identifies people by a random account ID rather than their email, and it can hold copies of records that were changed or deleted, including notes.

The app is designed not to hold identifying details. It blocks entries that look like phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, birth dates, Social Security numbers or medical record and insurance numbers. Please don't type real names into notes either.

On your device

Your phone's or browser's storage keeps you signed in, holds entries that haven't synced yet, and remembers which circles you've taken over in. Signing out clears all of this, and the app warns you first if anything hasn't been sent.

Technical information

Like any online service, our hosting providers receive basic technical information when your device connects, such as your IP address, device type and the time of the request, and keep it in short-lived server logs for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

  • To run the app: storing entries, syncing them to everyone in the circle and showing what's due.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • To email you sign-in codes and important account messages.
  • During the beta, to ask now and then how the app is working for you.
  • To send news about new features and plans, only if you ask for it. You can turn this off in the app's Account screen or with the unsubscribe link in any of those emails.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We don't sell or rent your information, or share it with advertising platforms or data brokers. We don't show ads or advertise other companies' products. The only promotional messages we send are our own news, to people who asked for it. We don't use what's logged in a circle to decide who gets those messages or what they say.

Who can see it

  • People in the circle. A circle's owners decide who is invited and what each person can see. Parents in the circle can also invite staff. Some entries can be limited to family only.
  • Service providers who run parts of the service for us and may use the information only to do so: Supabase (database and sign-in hosting), Resend (sending sign-in codes and other email), Cloudflare (the web version of the app and geteveryhand.com), GitHub (hosting friendsofelias.com), Google (our company email, when you write to us) and, once the app is in their stores, Apple and Google (app distribution).
  • When required by law, for example in response to a valid court order, or to protect someone's safety.
  • If the company changes hands, information could move to the new owner, who would have to follow this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep a circle's records while the circle exists so caregivers can look back at its history. Archiving a circle keeps everything.

  • If you delete your account in the app, your email, your sign-in and your circle memberships are removed from our live systems right away. If you ask us by email instead, we do it within 30 days. Entries you logged stay in each circle that carries on, so the child's record stays complete, under what that circle called you and no longer connected to your email.
  • Circles you run. If you're the only owner of a circle when you delete your account, the parent who joined it first becomes its owner. If no other parent is in it, the circle is deleted for everyone in it. The app shows which will happen to each of your circles before you confirm.
  • If a circle is deleted, we remove its entries, plans, members and invites from our live systems within 30 days.
  • Backups. Copies in our backups are removed as the backups expire, within a further 30 days.
  • The activity record is built so that it can't be edited, and it stays after an account or circle is deleted, including any copies of records it holds. Only we can reach it. It doesn't have a fixed end date yet.

Your choices

  • Delete your account in the app: My circles, then the person button, then Delete account. If you can't get into the app, or want a circle deleted, email privacy@friendsofelias.com from the address on your account.
  • Leave a circle, or, if you run it, remove people from it or archive it.
  • Turn off news emails in the app's Account screen.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing privacy@friendsofelias.com.

Security

Information is encrypted between your device and our servers and protected by access rules that let only a circle's members reach its records. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Health information and HIPAA

Everyhand is a tool for families. We are not a doctor, hospital or health plan, and the app isn't part of anyone's medical record, so in most cases HIPAA doesn't apply to it. We still treat your family's care information as sensitive and protect it as described here.

Children

The app is meant for adults who care for a child. It isn't meant to be used by children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Our websites

friendsofelias.com and geteveryhand.com use no cookies, analytics or trackers. Our website hosts receive the basic request information described above.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
